Terms and privacy

Plain language, no fine print. Last updated October 2026.

What you're buying

Each plan is a one-time payment for one song or one DJ mix. There is no subscription. What each plan includes is listed on the plans section of our homepage.

Delivery

After you pay, send us your song file, your streaming link, your cover art and a few details about the song. Your campaign is usually ready within 3 to 5 days of us receiving everything. It arrives as a private page with your plan, graphics and a PDF you can download.

Redos

If you're not happy with your promo materials, reply to your delivery email and tell us what's off. We'll redo them at no extra cost.

Refunds

If we haven't started on your campaign yet, email us and we'll refund you in full. Once your campaign has been delivered, we'll make it right with a redo instead.

What we don't do

  • We never buy streams, followers or plays, and never use bots. That breaks platform rules and can get your music taken down.
  • We can't promise a number of streams, playlist adds or followers. Nobody honestly can. We give you the plan, the materials and the strategy that give your song its best shot.

Your music stays yours

You keep all rights to your music, artwork and name. We only use what you send us to build your campaign. We'll only feature your work in our own posts if you say yes first.

Founding Artist special

20% off any plan for the first 20 orders that use the code FOUNDING20. One use per artist. It can't be combined with other offers, and it ends once 20 orders have used it.

Your information

  • What we collect: your name or artist name, email, the song details you send, and your files.
  • Why: only to build and deliver your campaign and to answer your questions.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Your delivery page: private. It isn't listed on search engines and only people you share the link with can see it.
  • We never sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.
  • Want it deleted? Email us and we'll remove your files and delivery page.
