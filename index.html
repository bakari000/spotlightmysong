Good to know

Do you guarantee a certain number of streams?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Guaranteed numbers usually mean bots, which can get your music taken down. We focus on promotion that reaches real listeners.

What platforms do you work with?

SoundCloud, Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Audiomack and more. If your song or DJ mix has a link, we can promote it.

Can I promote a DJ mix?

Yes. Mixes, singles, remixes, beats and full projects all work.

How fast do I get my campaign?

Most orders are delivered within 3 to 5 days after you send your music and cover art.

What comes in a Street Kit?

Posters, stickers and flyers designed for your release, each with a QR code that takes people straight to your song. It's printed and shipped to your door with a list of spots in your city to put them up.

Where should I put posters and stickers?

Anywhere that welcomes them: record stores, barbershops, coffee shops, venue and campus boards, open mics and your own shows. Always ask first. Promo on property without permission gets torn down and can get you fined.

What if I don't have cover art yet?

We can make it. A custom cover is 49 dollars for any release, with two rounds of revisions. Tap Request a cover in the plans above and tell us the vibe.
