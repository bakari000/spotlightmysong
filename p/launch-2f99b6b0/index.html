0 to 100 followers: weeks 1 and 2

  1. Post the 3 posts below in order, one every other day. Pin Post 1 and Post 2 to your profile.
  2. Repost your older content pack posts on the days in between, so the page never looks empty.
  3. Every day, spend 30 minutes in the scene (routine below). This is what gets the first 100. Posting alone won't.
  4. Ask 2MCH if you can feature his cover review as a before and after. Real customer work is the strongest post you can make.

The daily 30 minutes

  • 10 min: search hashtags like #independentartist, #undergroundrap, #newmusicfriday and #unsignedartist. Leave 10 real comments on artists' posts: something specific about the song or cover, not "fire 🔥".
  • 10 min: go to the comments under posts from bigger rap pages and music tip accounts. Reply helpfully to artists asking questions. People click on whoever gave the best answer.
  • 5 min: reply to every comment and DM you got. Fast replies get pushed more.
  • 5 min: post a story: a tip, a poll ("drop your next release date"), or a share of an artist you commented on.

Never use follow/unfollow bots, bought followers or mass cold DMs. Instagram restricts new accounts for it, and one ban kills the page.

100 to 1,000: weeks 3 to 8

  • Post 4 to 5 times a week: tips carousels from the weekly content packs, plus short Reels (text on screen over a beat, no face needed).
  • Every 2nd post ends with a comment keyword (CHECKLIST) so ManyChat turns comments into leads.
  • Do a weekly "cover art review" story where artists DM their cover and you pick 3 to review. It costs nothing and brings people back every week.
  • Once you have 3 to 5 posts doing better than the rest, boost the best one for $5 a day for 5 days, targeting ages 18 to 34 interested in DistroKid, BandLab, SoundCloud and home recording.
  • Post the same Reels on TikTok and YouTube Shorts. It takes zero extra work and doubles your reach.
